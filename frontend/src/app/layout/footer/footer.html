Spring Traffic Control — AG-UI & A2UI Demo

Disclaimer: This application is an educational demonstration of Agentic UI (AG-UI and A2UI) built with Spring Boot, Spring AI, and Angular.

All flight, station, and telemetry information displayed is simulated or sourced for demonstration purposes only and must not be used for real-world aviation navigation or air traffic control.

Note: Live OpenSky ATC callsigns may differ from commercial Schiphol flight numbers (e.g. deconflicted callsigns such as KLM163 for KL735). Unmatched callsigns are linked to Schiphol schedule data on a best-effort basis using airline, route, and time-window matching, so linked schedule details may occasionally be approximate or unavailable.
